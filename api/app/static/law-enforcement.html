♥ HeArt — Law Enforcement Protocols

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

1. Our commitment

HeArt prohibits human trafficking, sexual exploitation, and any aiding, abetting, or encouraging of abuse — see our Terms and Conditions and Acceptable Use Policy. HeArt cooperates with law enforcement to keep the service safe.

2. Reporting trafficking and exploitation

When HeArt becomes aware of suspected human trafficking or sexual exploitation, including any sexual content involving a minor, HeArt reports it to law enforcement and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline. Reports are made promptly. In an emergency, call 911. The National Human Trafficking Hotline is 1-888-373-7888.

3. Legal requests for information

HeArt responds to valid legal process — such as a subpoena, court order, or search warrant — as required by law. Requests should be sent to us through our contact page and must identify the legal authority, the account or content concerned, and the information requested. HeArt discloses only the information the legal process requires.

4. Emergency requests

Where law enforcement identifies an imminent risk of death or serious physical harm, HeArt will consider an emergency disclosure request and act as the law allows. Emergency requests should be sent through our contact page and marked as an emergency.

5. Preserving evidence

When HeArt receives a valid preservation request, or becomes aware of content that may be evidence of a crime, HeArt preserves the relevant account and content records as required by law and does not delete them while the request is in effect.

6. User reports

Any user can report abuse or suspected trafficking through our contact page. HeArt reviews reports, may remove content or accounts, and reports as described above.
