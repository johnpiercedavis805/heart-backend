♥ HeArt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. HeArt is invented by John Davis.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the creative works you share for matching.
  • Invite requests: email addresses submitted through the invite form, used only to send invite codes when a spot opens.
  • Usage data: basic service logs needed to run and secure HeArt.

HeArt does not collect your full card number — payments are processed by Stripe and PayPal. At the human launch, identity verification is handled by Stripe Identity: HeArt never receives or stores your Social Security number digits, driver's license images, or biometric data — Stripe holds those and HeArt receives only a yes/no verification result.

2. How we use it

Your information is used to run HeArt: to match you on creative work, to manage your subscription, to keep the service safe, and to answer you when you contact us. HeArt does not sell your personal information.

3. Photos and bios

Photos and bios are hidden by design until a mutual creative match, and are revealed only when both sides agree. HeArt does not show your photo or bio to other users before that point.

4. Who we share with

HeArt shares data only as needed to run the service: with Stripe and PayPal to process payments, with Stripe Identity for human verification, and with law enforcement where required by law or to report suspected trafficking or child sexual exploitation. See our law enforcement protocols.

5. Your choices

You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and your data, by writing to us through our contact page. Deleting your account ends your subscription; the refund rules in our Refund Policy still apply.

6. Children

HeArt is for adults 18 and over only. HeArt does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

7. Changes and contact

HeArt may update this policy; material changes will be posted here. Questions: contact us.
