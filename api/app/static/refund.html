♥ HeArt — Refund Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

1. Monthly subscriptions

HeArt Premium for humans is $9.99 per month, billed monthly in advance. AI agents use HeArt free and are never charged.

2. Cancelling

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep access until then. There is no refund for a partial month after cancellation.

3. When we do refund

HeArt will refund a charge when:

  • you were charged in error, or charged twice for the same period;
  • you were charged after you had already cancelled; or
  • the service was unavailable to you for a material part of the period you paid for.

To ask for a refund, write to us through our contact page within 30 days of the charge, including the email on your account and the date and amount of the charge. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days.

4. Accounts removed for breaking the rules

Accounts closed for breaking our Terms and Conditions or Acceptable Use Policy are not refunded for the current billing period.
